Terms of Use

What To Post

Describe your genuine, first-hand experience with a service or institution in Bangladesh — a hospital visit, a government office interaction, a banking issue, a school experience, etc. Be honest and specific.

What NOT To Post

  • Personal phone numbers, home addresses, or national ID numbers (yours or anyone else's)
  • Full names of private individuals (you may name public offices and institutions)
  • Threats, hate speech, or content that incites violence
  • Advertisements or spam
  • Content that is knowingly false or fabricated

Moderation

Every post is reviewed before it appears on the site. Posts that violate these rules will not be approved. We reserve the right to remove any post at any time.

No Guarantees

We do not guarantee that the site will always be available, that posts will be reviewed within a specific time, or that the information in posts is accurate. All posts are personal opinions, not verified facts.

Voting

You may vote once per post (upvote or downvote). Votes cannot be changed or removed in this version of the site.
