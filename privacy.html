Privacy

What We Do NOT Collect

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your IP address
  • Any account or login information (there are no accounts)

What Is Stored

When you submit a post, only the text you typed is saved: the title, category, institution name, location, star rating, and your experience. Nothing that identifies you.

What Is Stored in Your Browser

We use your browser's localStorage to remember which posts you have voted on (prevents double-voting) and your light/dark theme preference. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Moderation

All posts are reviewed before they appear on the site. This is done to filter out spam, threats, and content that contains personal information like phone numbers or home addresses.

Third-Party Services

Posts and votes are stored on a secure, hosted server. No analytics, ads, or tracking scripts are used.
