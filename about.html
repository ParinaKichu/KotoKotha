About KotoKotha

KotoKotha (কতকথা) is an anonymous platform where people in Bangladesh can share their real experiences with government offices, hospitals, banks, schools, internet providers, and other services.

The idea is simple: if you visited a place and had a good or bad experience, write it down here so others know what to expect.

How It Works

  • Share — Describe your experience with any service or institution. Pick a category, location, and star rating.
  • Review — Every post is checked before it goes live, to filter out spam and harmful content.
  • Vote — Agree with someone's experience? Upvote it. Disagree? Downvote it.

What "Anonymous" Means

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any account. There is no login and no sign-up. You simply write and submit.
